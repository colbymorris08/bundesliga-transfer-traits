Key Findings

Demonstration Paradox

More protests = incumbent more likely to survive (coef: -1.02). Regimes facing protests entrench rather than concede.

Legitimacy, Not Fragility

FSI Total is negative (-0.26), but FSI Legitimacy alone is +1.21. It's the perception of illegitimacy that drives change.

Russia vs China

Russian-backed states: 4.5% turnover. Chinese-invested states: 9.8% (average). Russia protects rulers; China doesn't care who wins.
